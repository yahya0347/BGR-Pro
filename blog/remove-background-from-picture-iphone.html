How to Remove Background from a Picture on iPhone

To remove the background from a picture on iPhone, open the photo, touch and hold the subject until it's outlined, then tap Copy or Share to lift it — this uses iOS's built-in Visual Look Up feature and works without installing any app. For faster results or backgrounds that need transparency saved as a PNG, an AI background remover like EraserPro handles it in one upload.

Method 1 — Using the Built-In iOS Feature (No App Needed)

  1. Open the photo in the Photos app.
  2. Touch and hold on the main subject until a white outline (shimmer effect) appears around it.
  3. Lift your finger, then tap Copy to copy just the subject, or tap the Share icon to save it as a new image.
  4. Paste it into another app (Messages, Notes, Mail) or save it directly.

Limitation: this works subject-by-subject and doesn't always export a clean transparent PNG — it's built for quick copy-paste, not final transparent files.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to download an app to remove a background on iPhone?

No — iOS has a built-in feature (touch and hold), and AI tools like EraserPro work directly in Safari without installing anything.

Can I save the background-removed photo as a transparent PNG on iPhone?

The built-in iOS feature doesn't always export a clean transparent PNG; an AI background remover gives you a proper transparent file to download.

Does this work on older iPhones?

The touch-and-hold subject lift works on iPhones running iOS 16 or later; AI-based tools work on any iPhone with a browser, regardless of iOS version.

Is removing a background on iPhone free?

Yes — both the built-in iOS feature and EraserPro's tool are free to use.
