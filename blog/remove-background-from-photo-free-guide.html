Why People Remove Backgrounds From Photos

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Starting from a low-resolution or blurry photo. The AI can only work with the detail that's actually in the source image — a sharp original gives a cleaner cutout.
  • Skipping the edge check. Always zoom in on hair, fur, or semi-transparent areas (like glass or smoke) before downloading — these are the hardest regions for any tool, AI or manual, to get perfect.
  • Re-saving as JPG. JPG doesn't support transparency, so a transparent cutout saved as JPG will get a solid background baked back in. Always export as PNG if you need the transparency to survive.
  • Using photos you don't have the rights to. Background removal is meant for your own images or ones you're licensed to edit — not for reworking someone else's copyrighted photo.
