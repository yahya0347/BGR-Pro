Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about EraserPro AI

How does the AI background remover work?
Our AI background remover automatically detects the main subject in your photo and removes the background instantly using advanced machine learning models, ensuring high-quality, clean cutouts.
Is EraserPro AI completely free to use?
Yes, EraserPro AI offers free background removal and watermark eraser features. You can upload images and process them without a paid subscription.
How do I erase watermarks from my images without losing quality?
To remove watermarks, select the Watermark Eraser tool, adjust your brush size, paint over the watermark text or logo, and click 'Erase Watermark'. The AI reconstruction model fills in the background details seamlessly.
Can I add custom text or image watermarks to my photos?
Yes! The Add Watermark (Watermark Maker) tool allows you to upload custom text logos or images, adjust their transparency, size, and tile patterns to protect your creative work.
Does EraserPro AI support PDF document pages?
Absolutely. You can import PDF pages directly into the workspace, extract pages as images, edit them using our background and watermark tools, and export them back to your device.
Are my uploaded photos safe and private?
Yes, privacy is our top priority. AI image processing (for background removal and AI watermark erasing) is performed securely via APIs and your image data is not stored after processing. For all other tools, processing is done entirely on your device and never sent to any server.
