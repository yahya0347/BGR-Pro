How to Remove Watermarks from Your Own Photos and Files

Whether it's an old company stamp on an archived photo, a timestamp overlay from a camera, or a preview mark on a stock image you've already licensed, this tool reconstructs the pixels underneath a watermark automatically.

Use this tool only on images you own or have the rights to edit — it's built for cleaning up your own content, not for removing copyright protection from someone else's work.

Common Use Cases

  • Removing an old logo overlay from your own archived photos
  • Cleaning up personal photos with a date/timestamp stamp
  • Removing a preview watermark from a stock image after purchasing the license
  • Removing your own outdated watermark before re-branding — pair it with our Watermark Maker to add your new one.

Tips

  • Larger, higher-resolution source images give a cleaner reconstruction.
  • Works best on watermarks over relatively simple or textured backgrounds; very busy backgrounds may need a manual touch-up afterward.
